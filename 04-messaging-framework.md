# Messaging Framework: How Pfizer Talks About Padcev

**Deliverable 04 of 07**
**Project:** Padcev First-Line Launch Strategy
**Purpose:** Establish the approved messaging hierarchy, clinical claims architecture, audience-specific messaging variants, and the regulatory compliance principles that govern all promotional communications

---

## A Note on Pharmaceutical Messaging Compliance

Every message in this framework must survive the MLR (Medical, Legal, Regulatory) review process. That means every efficacy claim must be directly supported by data in the FDA-approved prescribing information or peer-reviewed published literature, with appropriate context and statistical caveats. Every safety reference must include fair balance. No comparative claims against competitors unless those comparisons are based on head-to-head trial data.

This is a PMM portfolio project, not a reviewed and approved promotional piece. The messages below are directionally accurate and clinically grounded but would require full MLR review before use in any Pfizer promotional context.

## The Brand Essence

Before the hierarchy, there is the brand positioning statement. This is internal-facing and guides all external messaging.

**For oncologists treating first-line locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma patients, Padcev (enfortumab vedotin-ejfv) in combination with pembrolizumab is the first and only treatment to demonstrate a doubling of median overall survival compared to platinum-based chemotherapy, delivering a 29% complete response rate in all-comers regardless of PD-L1 status or cisplatin eligibility, because Padcev's targeted Nectin-4 ADC mechanism directly attacks the urothelial cancer cells that IO therapy and chemotherapy cannot reliably eliminate.**

That positioning statement will never appear on a piece of promotional literature. But it is the north star that all external messages point toward.

## Messaging Hierarchy Level 1: The Core Campaign Platform

This is the message that appears across all branded promotional channels: print, digital, sales aid, patient-facing materials, and convention booth.

**Primary headline:** "Defeat UC from the start."

**Supporting claim:** "Padcev + Keytruda: the only combination proven to reduce the risk of death by 53% compared to platinum-based chemotherapy in first-line urothelial carcinoma."

**Data anchor:** Hazard ratio of 0.47 from EV-302/KEYNOTE-A39 (95% CI: 0.38-0.58; p less than 0.00001).

**The choice of "Defeat":** The word signals completeness and conviction. It aligns with the complete response data (29.1% CR rate) and differentiates from "control" or "manage," which are the functional language of IO monotherapy and chemotherapy.

**Fair balance note:** All branded messages require inclusion of the Important Safety Information (ISI) and reference to the full Prescribing Information. Key safety information that must accompany efficacy claims includes neuropathy, skin reactions, hyperglycemia, and pneumonitis.

## Messaging Hierarchy Level 2: The Three Pillars

Every promotional material, digital touchpoint, and sales conversation builds from three clinical messages. Each pillar is supported by specific data points from the EV-302 trial.

### Pillar 1: Unmatched Survival Improvement

**Pillar statement:** "Padcev plus pembrolizumab nearly doubled median overall survival compared to chemotherapy in first-line urothelial carcinoma."

**Supporting data:**
- Median OS: 31.5 months (EV+P) versus 16.1 months (chemotherapy)
- Hazard ratio: 0.47 (95% CI: 0.38-0.58)
- Reduction in risk of death: 53%

**Why this pillar leads:** OS is the gold standard endpoint in oncology. Payers, oncologists, and patients all understand what survival means. The magnitude of improvement (15.4 months) is exceptional by any historical comparison in this disease and in most solid tumors.

**Audience-specific framing:**
- For the oncologist: "The HR of 0.47 in EV-302 represents one of the largest OS improvements ever demonstrated in a first-line bladder cancer randomized phase 3 trial."
- For the payer: "Patients on EV plus pembrolizumab lived nearly twice as long as those on chemotherapy. That survival extension translates directly to quality-adjusted life years."
- For the patient (plain language): "In the clinical trial, people who received Padcev with pembrolizumab lived, on average, about 15 months longer than those who received chemotherapy."

### Pillar 2: Responses That Last

**Pillar statement:** "Padcev plus pembrolizumab achieved a 67.7% objective response rate with a 29.1% complete response rate — more than double the complete response rate of chemotherapy."

**Supporting data:**
- ORR: 67.7% (EV+P) versus 44.4% (chemotherapy)
- CR rate: 29.1% (EV+P) versus 12.5% (chemotherapy)
- Duration of response: Ongoing responders at the time of data cutoff included patients with responses exceeding 24 months

**Why this pillar matters:** Response rate data matters to oncologists because it tells them what happens at the first imaging scan. An oncologist who starts a patient on a new regimen needs confidence that the majority of their patients will respond. A 67.7% ORR means roughly two out of three patients will have measurable tumor shrinkage. The 29.1% CR rate means nearly one in three patients will achieve no detectable tumor.

**The complete response emphasis:** Complete response is the strongest language available in oncology without claiming cure. A patient who achieves CR has no detectable cancer on imaging. That framing resonates powerfully with both physicians and patients. The doubling of the CR rate compared to chemotherapy is the most impactful individual data point in the entire EV-302 dataset for messaging purposes.

### Pillar 3: Benefit Across All Patients

**Pillar statement:** "The survival benefit of Padcev plus pembrolizumab was consistent regardless of PD-L1 status, cisplatin eligibility, or presence of liver metastases."

**Supporting data:**
- Cisplatin-ineligible subgroup: HR 0.52 for OS
- PD-L1 CPS less than 10 subgroup: HR 0.45 for OS
- Liver metastases subgroup: Benefit maintained
- All prespecified subgroups showed consistent benefit

**Why this pillar differentiates from IO-only approaches:** Keytruda's 1L approval for cisplatin-ineligible patients was restricted to PD-L1 CPS 10 or higher. There are no approved treatment options with proven survival benefit for cisplatin-ineligible patients with PD-L1 CPS below 10. EV-302 shows Padcev plus pembrolizumab works regardless of PD-L1 status. This is a direct and permissible commercial message that does not require a direct comparative claim against Keytruda.

## Messaging Hierarchy Level 3: Audience-Specific Messages

### Message Set A: For Urologic Oncologists and GU Medical Oncologists

These are the high-science prescribers. They have read the EV-302 paper in the New England Journal of Medicine. They want mechanism, clinical detail, and subgroup data.

**Lead message:** "The Nectin-4 ADC mechanism of enfortumab vedotin directly targets urothelial cancer cells regardless of biomarker status, delivering cytotoxic payload through receptor-mediated endocytosis while the bystander effect addresses tumor heterogeneity. In combination with pembrolizumab, this Nectin-4-directed killing creates immunogenic cell death that may amplify the checkpoint inhibitor response."

**Supporting scientific points:**
- Nectin-4 is expressed in 97% or more of urothelial carcinoma tumors (high, homogeneous expression)
- Enfortumab vedotin's MMAE payload has a bystander-killing effect that addresses intratumoral heterogeneity
- The immunogenic cell death hypothesis may explain why adding EV to pembrolizumab improves outcomes even in patients with low PD-L1 expression

**Practical clinical messages for this audience:**
- "Dose modification guidance for neuropathy management is in the prescribing information and has been validated in clinical practice"
- "Patients do not require PD-L1 testing or any other companion diagnostic before prescribing EV plus pembrolizumab"
- "The co-administration of Padcev with pembrolizumab does not require changes to pembrolizumab dosing or schedule"

### Message Set B: For General Community Oncologists

These physicians want clinical relevance and practical simplicity, not deep mechanistic detail.

**Lead message:** "For your patients with locally advanced or metastatic bladder cancer, Padcev plus Keytruda is now the preferred first-line treatment regardless of how fit they are for cisplatin or what their PD-L1 status is."

**Supporting practical messages:**
- "No biomarker testing required"
- "NCCN Category 1 preferred for all first-line la/mUC patients"
- "Dose modification guidelines are well-established from the 2L+ experience"

### Message Set C: For Payer Medical Directors and P&T Committees

These audiences evaluate evidence based on clinical and economic value, not promotion.

**Lead message:** "EV-302 demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in overall survival that meets or exceeds the threshold for clinically superior outcomes in a serious and life-threatening condition."

**Economic messages:**
- "At 31.5 months median OS versus 16.1 months, patients on EV plus pembrolizumab live substantially longer and spend substantially less time cycling through subsequent lines of systemic therapy"
- "The 29.1% complete response rate, with some durable responders at 24+ months, suggests a proportion of patients may have substantially reduced long-term treatment costs"
- "ICER (Institute for Clinical and Economic Review) analysis has assessed similar ADC combinations as cost-effective at standard oncology QALY thresholds"

### Message Set D: For Patients and Caregivers (Plain Language)

Pharma PMM is responsible for patient-facing materials. These require plain language at a 6th-to-8th-grade reading level, cultural sensitivity, and empathetic tone. The FDA requires that patient materials be reviewed for accuracy and not promote the drug beyond its approved indication.

**Core patient message:** "Padcev works differently from chemotherapy. It is designed to find and enter bladder cancer cells and destroy them directly. When Padcev is given with Keytruda, together they work in two ways at the same time to fight cancer: Padcev goes after the cancer cells directly, and Keytruda helps your immune system fight cancer."

**Survival data in plain language:** "In a large clinical trial with 886 patients, people who received Padcev with Keytruda lived, on average, about 31 and a half months. People who received standard chemotherapy lived about 16 months. That means people on Padcev with Keytruda lived about 15 and a half months longer on average."

**Key safety information for patients:** The patient-facing ISI must include prominently: neuropathy (numbness, tingling, pain in hands and feet), skin reactions, high blood sugar, serious lung problems, and infusion-related reactions. Patients should be instructed to contact their oncology team immediately if they develop any of these.

## Messages to Avoid: The Compliance Guardrails

**Do not say:** "Padcev is better than Keytruda." This is a comparative claim that is not supported by a head-to-head randomized trial against Keytruda monotherapy in this specific population.

**Do not say:** "Padcev cures bladder cancer." CR data is not the same as cure, and making that claim is outside the label.

**Do not say:** "Padcev is safe." No drug is safe. The appropriate language is "the safety profile of Padcev plus pembrolizumab was manageable and consistent with the established profiles of each agent."

**Do not say:** "Every patient should receive Padcev." There are patients who cannot access ADC therapy, who have contraindications, or for whom carboplatin-based regimens remain appropriate. Absolute statements are out of compliance.

**Do not imply:** That the EV-302 data can be directly compared to KEYNOTE-052 or CheckMate 901 data as if it were a head-to-head randomized comparison. Cross-trial comparisons are not appropriate promotional claims.

## Message Testing Framework

Before finalizing the campaign, messaging should be tested with oncologists and patients using a structured market research approach:

**Testing methodology:** Concept testing with 25 to 30 target oncologists (mix of Segments A and B) using aided and unaided message recall, message believability ratings, and clinical importance rankings.

**Key messages to test:**
1. The "defeats UC from the start" headline (brand resonance)
2. The 53% reduction in risk of death claim (medical accuracy perception)
3. The "regardless of PD-L1 status" message (differentiation value)
4. The complete response rate emphasis (motivational impact on prescribing intent)

**Target outcomes for each message:** 70% or more unprompted recall at 48 hours; 80% or more "clinically relevant" rating; 60% or more prescribing intent improvement in PD-L1-low cisplatin-ineligible patient scenarios.
